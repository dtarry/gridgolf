How to play
Move your ball from the Tee (TE) to the Hole (H) in as few strokes as possible.
Roll the die, then click a highlighted cell to move.
Fairway/Tee: +1 to roll  ·  Bunker: −1 to roll
Trees & Water: fly over only — cannot land on them
From fairway only: path may cross trees
Slope: ball slides 1 square in arrow direction after landing
Putt: move 1 square any time, even after rolling
Mulligan: re-roll the die — 6 shared per round
Par is 6 per hole · 18 holes · lowest total score wins
